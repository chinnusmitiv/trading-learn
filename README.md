## 🔍 Module Overviews

### 1. [Smart Money Concepts (SMC)](SMC/SMC_trading_plan.md)
A modular, step-by-step 28-week curriculum built on institutional order flow logic rather than pattern matching.

* **[Phase 1: Market Structure (Weeks 1–4)](SMC/SMC_trading_plan.md#phase-1-market-structure-weeks-14)**
  * Swing highs/lows (HH, HL, LH, LL) and trend identification
  * Break of Structure (**BOS**) vs. Change of Character (**CHoCH**)
  * Multi-timeframe structure alignment (Daily → 4H → 1H)
* **[Phase 2: Liquidity (Weeks 5–8)](SMC/SMC_trading_plan.md#phase-2-liquidity-weeks-58)**
  * Buy-side liquidity (BSL) and Sell-side liquidity (SSL)
  * Liquidity sweeps / stop hunts and institutional displacement
  * Inducements and determining the Draw on Liquidity
* **[Phase 3: Order Blocks & FVGs (Weeks 9–14)](SMC/SMC_trading_plan.md#phase-3-order-blocks--fvgs-weeks-914)**
  * Displacement identification and Fair Value Gaps (FVG)
  * Order block (OB) mitigation, breaker blocks, and OB grading
  * Dealing ranges, equilibrium, and Premium vs. Discount pricing
* **[Phase 4: Entry Model & Backtesting (Weeks 15–20)](SMC/SMC_trading_plan.md#phase-4-putting-it-together--entry-model-weeks-1520)**
  * Repeatable entry sequence: `HTF Bias → Liquidity Sweep → Zone Confluence → LTF CHoCH → Entry`
  * Defined Stop Loss, Take Profit, and Risk-to-Reward rules
  * 50+ trade backtesting sample size and expectancy analysis
* **[Phase 5: Paper Trading & Micro Live (Weeks 21–28)](SMC/SMC_trading_plan.md#phase-5-paper-trading--live-validation-weeks-2128)**
  * Simulation on TradingView / broker demo
  * Statistical validation (win rate ≥ 70% or profit factor ≥ 1.5, max drawdown < 10%)
  * Micro-lot live execution to build psychological discipline
* **[Phase 6: Ongoing Improvement (Month 7+)](SMC/SMC_trading_plan.md#phase-6-ongoing-improvement-month-7)**
  * Rigorous trade journaling, order flow confluence (DOM / Footprint), and refinement

## 🎯 Recommended Study Routine

1. **Master One Concept at a Time**: Complete each phase's weekly chart drills before moving to the next.
2. **Log Every Drill**: Keep chart screenshots of both successful setups and failures.
3. **Internalize the Logic**: Remember the foundational rule:
   > *Structure tells you the direction. Liquidity tells you the target. Order blocks and FVGs tell you where to enter. LTF confirmation tells you when. Risk management keeps you alive.*
4. **Pass Mastery Tests**: Each section of the [SMC Trading Plan](SMC/SMC_trading_plan.md) contains specific benchmark criteria to assess readiness before progressing.
