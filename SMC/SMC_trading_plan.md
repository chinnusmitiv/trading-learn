## SMC Learning Roadmap

Total estimated time: **4–6 months** of consistent study (8–11 hrs/week). The rule is **modular** — master one concept before adding the next.

---

### Phase 1: Market Structure (Weeks 1–4)

**Goal:** Read the chart like a map.

| Week | Focus | Drill |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | HH / HL / LH / LL, trend identification | Open a Daily chart. Mark every swing high and low. Label the trend. Do 10 charts. |
| 2 | **BOS** (Break of Structure) — trend continuation | On the same charts, mark every candle that breaks the prior swing. Verify: did the trend continue after? |
| 3 | **CHoCH** (Change of Character) — trend reversal | Find 20 historical reversals. Mark the exact candle that broke structure against the trend. |
| 4 | Multi-timeframe structure (Daily → 4H → 1H) | Pick one market. Mark structure on all three. Note where they align or conflict. |

**Resources:**
- ICT's free "Market Structure" videos on his YouTube (the 2023 Mentorship text is the canonical source)
- *Technical Analysis of Financial Markets* by John Murphy (chapters on trend analysis)

**Mastery test:** You can open any chart, any timeframe, and correctly label BOS vs CHoCH within 30 seconds without hesitation.

---

### Phase 2: Liquidity (Weeks 5–8)

**Goal:** Understand *where* price is going and *why*.

| Week | Focus | Drill |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Buy-side liquidity (BSL) & Sell-side liquidity (SSL) | Mark all equal highs (BSL) and equal lows (SSL) on a weekly chart. Note which ones got swept and which didn't. |
| 6 | **Liquidity sweeps / stop hunts** | Find 20 examples of price wicking through a level and reversing. Mark the sweep. Ask: was there displacement after? |
| 7 | **Inducement** — fake breakouts that trap traders | Identify 10 instances where price broke a level, trapped traders, then reversed. What was the structure before the trap? |
| 8 | **Draw on liquidity** — identifying the target | For 10 historical moves, identify the liquidity pool that price was "heading toward" before the move started. |

**Key insight to internalize:** Price moves *toward* liquidity. The "target" of any move is the nearest pool of resting stops.

**Mastery test:** Given a chart with no annotations, you can point to the nearest BSL/SSL and predict which one price is likely to target next.

---

### Phase 3: Order Blocks & FVGs (Weeks 9–14)

**Goal:** Identify *where* institutions entered and where price will react.

| Week | Focus | Drill |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Displacement** — what makes a move "institutional" | Mark 20 strong impulsive moves. What do they have in common? (Large candles, breaking structure, leaving gaps) |
| 10 | **Order Blocks (OB)** — last opposing candle before displacement | Mark 30 OBs. Track which ones got "mitigated" (price returned) and which were broken. Note the context. |
| 11 | **Fair Value Gaps (FVG)** — 3-candle imbalances | Mark 30 FVGs. Which ones got filled? Which were left unfilled? What separated them? |
| 12 | **OB grading** — not all OBs are equal | Classify your marked OBs: fresh vs. tapped, with displacement vs. without, in premium vs. discount. Track hit rate. |
| 13 | **Breaker blocks** — failed OBs that flip | Find 10 examples. What happens after a breaker forms? |
| 14 | **Premium / Discount** — dealing range & 50% equilibrium | Draw the dealing range on 10 charts. Mark the 50% line. Note: do OBs in discount get filled more reliably than OBs in premium? |

**Mastery test:** You can look at a displacement move and immediately identify the OB, the FVG it left, and whether it's in premium or discount — all within 10 seconds.

---

### Phase 4: Putting It Together — Entry Model (Weeks 15–20)

**Goal:** Build one repeatable, rules-based setup.

The standard SMC entry sequence:

```
HTF Bias → Liquidity Sweep → OB/FVG in Discount/Premium → LTF CHoCH → Entry
```

| Week | Focus | Drill |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | **HTF bias + zone mapping** (Daily/4H) | Pick a market. Mark: (1) trend direction, (2) key OB/FVG zones, (3) nearest liquidity pools. Write a 3-sentence "bias statement." |
| 16 | **Wait for the sweep + zone confluence** | Replay 20 sessions. Identify moments where price swept liquidity AND entered an HTF zone simultaneously. |
| 17 | **LTF confirmation** (5M/1M CHoCH) | For each confluence from Week 16, drop to 5M. Did a CHoCH confirm? Mark the entry candle. |
| 18 | **Entry, SL, TP rules** | Define: Entry = LTF CHoCH close. SL = beyond the sweep wick. TP = opposing liquidity pool. Calculate R:R for each. |
| 19 | **Backtesting** (50+ trades) | Go back 6 months. Apply your model. Log every trade: entry, SL, TP, result, R:R. Calculate win rate and expectancy. |
| 20 | **Refinement** | Review your 50 trades. What's the #1 reason for losses? Adjust one rule. Re-test 20 more. |

**Mastery test:** You can execute your model from start to finish on a live chart in under 2 minutes without second-guessing.

---

### Phase 5: Paper Trading & Live Validation (Weeks 21–28)

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 21–24 | **Demo trading** — trade your model on a simulator (TradingView paper trading, or broker demo). Log every trade. |
| 25–26 | **Review** — 30+ demo trades minimum. Calculate: win rate, avg R:R, max drawdown, profit factor. |
| 27–28 | **Micro live** — trade with minimum size (0.01 lot / 1 contract). The goal is *psychological*, not financial. |

**Go-live criteria (all must be met):**
- ≥ 70% win rate OR ≥ 1.5 profit factor over 50+ trades
- Max drawdown < 10% of account
- You can explain *why* every trade you took in one sentence
- You've traded at least 20 live micro trades without breaking your rules

---

### Phase 6: Ongoing Improvement (Month 7+)

| Area | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Journaling** | Every trade: screenshot, bias, zone, entry trigger, result, emotional state. Review weekly. |
| **One concept at a time** | Add one refinement per month (e.g., kill zones, session timing, volume confirmation) |
| **Order flow (optional)** | Once SMC is solid, layer in footprint/DOM to confirm absorption at your zones |
| **Community** | r/Forex, r/DayTrading, or a dedicated SMC Discord — share analysis, get feedback |

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why it kills you |
|---|---|
| Marking every candle as an OB | Dilutes the concept; you'll enter everything |
| Trading without HTF bias | You'll fade strong trends and get run over |
| Entering on zone touch without LTF confirmation | The zone is a *location*, not a *signal* |
| Skipping backtesting | You'll discover your model doesn't work after losing real money |
| Changing your model every losing trade | You need 50+ trades before any conclusion is valid |
| Ignoring the "why" and just pattern-matching | SMC is a *logic framework*, not a checklist |

---

### One-Sentence Summary

> **Structure tells you the direction. Liquidity tells you the target. Order blocks and FVGs tell you where to enter. LTF confirmation tells you when. Risk management keeps you alive.**

Master each in sequence. Don't skip ahead. The first 4 weeks of just marking BOS/CHoCH will save you months of confusion later.
